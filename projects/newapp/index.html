Constraint as strategy

Less surface area. More truth.

Instead of bolting on “startup features” before the core experience is proven, v1 deliberately removes them. The absence of paywalls, login, analytics, social graphs and checkout is part of the product contract.

What intentionally does not

StoreKit, subscriptions, paywalls, trial copy, restore-purchase UI, affiliate checkout, login, sync, social features, tracking SDKs and notifications are all outside this v1 boundary.

Evidence corpus

Provider behavior is recorded and checked rather than assumed from a successful JSON response.

Release firewall

Static audits and release scripts reject obvious App Review risks before a build can be treated as ready.

Real device still wins

A passing source audit is explicitly not treated as proof that the final app works on target hardware.

The experiment behind the experiment

Can discipline be a feature?

NEWAPP is a test of whether a product gets stronger when architecture prevents the team from pretending a capability exists before the evidence does.
