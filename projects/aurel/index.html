Editorial commerce · Discovery

AUREL

A beauty storefront designed like a curated edit rather than a fake marketplace, starting with researched KIKO MILANO complexion and gloss products.

Drop 001

10 researched products. No fake checkout.

The first release focuses on five complexion formulas and five gloss-forward lip products, linking outward to official retailer pages instead of pretending the site processes commerce it does not own.

Design language

Warm neutrals, berry accents, oversized editorial typography, real product imagery, restrained motion and responsive layouts. The goal is magazine energy with storefront utility.

Commerce model

The product is structured for affiliate discovery. Tracking links can replace retailer URLs later without redesigning the experience, while the interface avoids implying official brand affiliation.

Current shape

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript keep this phase intentionally direct: no backend, accounts, local cart, checkout, database or payment processing. That absence is a product decision, not an unfinished fake feature.
