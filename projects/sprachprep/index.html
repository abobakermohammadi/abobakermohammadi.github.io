01 · Education · Web + iOS

SprachPrep

A German exam-preparation product built around a simple shift: stop “studying German” in the abstract and start training the exact muscles an exam asks you to use.

The learning loop
Practice → feedback → progress → exam readiness.
ReadingListeningWritingSpeakingMock examsProgress
Flagship productConsumer learning system

Product thesis

Train for the moment that matters.

SprachPrep is organized around exam readiness rather than a generic content warehouse. Practice loops, visible progress and skill-specific surfaces make the product feel closer to training than browsing.

SurfaceResponsive web product
CompanionNative iOS work
Core loopPractice + feedback + progress

Four skills, one system.

The experience has to make reading, listening, writing and speaking feel related without pretending they are the same task.

Read→Listen→Write→Speak

Progress has a job.

Hearts, XP, streaks and visible progress are not there to decorate lessons. They create pacing, return reasons and a sense that practice accumulates into something measurable.

Practice

Grammar, vocabulary and skill work give users a place to train outside full mock exams.

Simulation

Mock-exam flows turn isolated practice into exam-like sequences and pressure.

Release reality

Accounts, subscriptions, recovery, App Store constraints and entitlement states are part of making the learning product real.

Why this one matters most

Content is only half the product.

The hard part is the web between curriculum, product state, motivation, billing, platform rules and reliability. SprachPrep is where those systems collide, which is why it sits at the center of this portfolio.
