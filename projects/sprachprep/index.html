Education · Web + iOS

SprachPrep

A German exam-preparation product built around practice that feels active: skills, mock exams, speaking, progress systems, gamification and a native iOS companion.

Flagship product

From “study German” to “train for the exam.”

The product is organized around exam readiness rather than a generic content library, with practice loops, visible progress and multiple skill surfaces.

Learning system

  • Reading, listening, writing and speaking practice
  • Grammar and vocabulary work
  • Mock exam flows
  • Hearts, XP, streaks and progress feedback
  • Exam-focused user journeys

Product surface

SprachPrep spans a responsive web product and native iOS work, with subscription infrastructure, onboarding, account flows and the less glamorous release details that turn a prototype into a real consumer product.

Why it is the flagship

This is where product, curriculum, monetization, platform constraints, App Store requirements, user progress and reliability all meet. It is not one feature. It is an operating learning product that has had to survive real release pressure.
