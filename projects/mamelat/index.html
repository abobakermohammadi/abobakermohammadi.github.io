02 · Real estate · Istanbul

MAMELAT

A Turkish-first property experience designed around one conversion rule: make the business understandable, make the next action obvious, then get out of the customer's way.

Public path
Understand → choose → WhatsApp or call.

Product rule

Zero fake urgency. Zero fake inventory.

The current public experience is useful even when no listing is published. It refuses the easiest real-estate tricks: fabricated stock, fake testimonials, invented statistics and decorative “luxury” noise.

The owner side

The admin is intentionally described for what it is: a localhost/browser-local console for listings, image previews, leads and settings. It does not pretend to be a shared cloud CMS.

Empty by design

Fresh sessions start without seeded public inventory. A missing listing becomes an honest state, not a reason to fabricate content.

Brand restraint

Forest green, antique gold and ivory give the product identity without swallowing the property itself.

Motion with an off switch

Perspective and light-sweep details exist, but reduced-motion users keep all content and navigation intact.

The interesting constraint

How do you make “nothing listed” still feel intentional?

That question forced the product away from a listing-grid template and toward the actual business relationship: explain the service, route the intent, make contact immediate, and let inventory appear only when it is real.
