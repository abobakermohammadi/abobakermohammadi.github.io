Real estate · Istanbul

MAMELAT

A quiet, Turkish-first property experience built around one conversion path: understand the business, choose what you need, then WhatsApp or call.

Product rule

Less browsing. More certainty.

The current public experience intentionally avoids fake inventory, fake testimonials and fake statistics. It stays useful even with zero listings published.

Public experience

  • Brand-first homepage
  • Buyer and seller pathways
  • Direct WhatsApp and call handoff
  • Future-proof listing and detail routes
  • Private/off-market request as a secondary path

Owner side

A localhost-only owner console handles listing create/edit/delete, image previews, leads, contact settings and workflow state without pretending browser-local data is a shared cloud CMS.

Design system

Forest green, antique gold and ivory form a restrained identity. Motion is optional and reduced-motion aware. The project deliberately prioritizes legibility, contact speed and trust over decorative real-estate clichés.
