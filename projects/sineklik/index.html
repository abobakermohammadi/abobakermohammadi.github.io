Local business · Full stack

Sineklik
İstanbul

A local-service website that grew into a business system: discovery, configuration, appointments, messaging, quotes, portal and admin in one product.

The idea

Turn “what do I need?” into a guided flow.

Visitors can discover suitable products instead of decoding industry terminology, then move directly into a quote, appointment or conversation.

Customer side

  • Interactive product showroom
  • “Sineklik Bul” recommendation flow
  • Visual configurator
  • Appointment and status flows
  • Realtime messaging via SSE
  • Quotes and customer portal

Business side

  • Phone-first admin panel
  • Authenticated sessions
  • Availability and pricing logic
  • Protected uploads
  • Lead and customer workflows

Engineering

Built with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React 19, Prisma and SQLite by default, with a path to PostgreSQL. The repository documents unit tests and end-to-end Playwright coverage for core flows and responsive behavior.
