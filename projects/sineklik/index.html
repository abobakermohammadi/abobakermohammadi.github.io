Pricing refuses to guess

Rules begin inactive. Without configured real pricing, the customer gets a discovery CTA instead of an invented estimate.

Uploads are protected

Private files are validated, renamed and served through access-controlled routes rather than thrown into a public folder.

Auth has layers

Admin access is checked through session cookies, middleware/layout protections and API-level authorization.

The architectural shift

Marketing site → business workflow.

Once appointments, messaging, quotes, customer status and admin exist in the same product, the website is no longer an ad for the business. It starts becoming part of how the business operates.
